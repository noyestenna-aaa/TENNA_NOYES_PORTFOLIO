Research / Astronomy

New Astrometric Measurements of STF 1985

Journal of Double Star Observations (JDSO)

This study measured the mean position angle and separation of WDS 15559-0210 to investigate whether the system's orbit had observably changed since its last published position. The measurements showed a modest change: separation shifted from 6.16″ to 5.7″, while position angle advanced from 354.5° to 355.61°. These findings are consistent with previous observations.

Technical Communication / Research Proposal

A Research Proposal on Improving API Technical Documentation

Qualitative research design

This proposal investigates which features of API documentation help developers use interfaces correctly and efficiently. It asks how two selected API documents demonstrate completeness, clarity, code-oriented support, and organization; where developers succeed or encounter confusion when following them; and how document features may explain those outcomes.

The proposed study has three stages: evaluate two public API documents using a literature-informed rubric; obtain IRB approval and observe six programmers using one document in individual sessions, followed by interviews; then code and compare interview findings with the rubric results. The findings could help API writers create documentation that better supports developers and software engineers.

Literary Analysis / Cultural Criticism

Themes of the Objectified Feminine within Culture

Literary analysis of Maxine Hong Kingston’s The Woman Warrior

Maxine Hong Kingston’s The Woman Warrior is a memoir like no other, violently and shamelessly sharing Kingston’s experience as a girl and woman born in a Chinese American immigrant family. She illustrates her experience through fantasy retellings, ghost stories, and her personal history. The Woman Warrior examines themes of assimilation and the price that women pay for being born as girls. Women are shamed for being raped, shamed for having privacy, and objectified and judged from the moment they come into this life. Kingston’s memoir shows the brutal reality of how American and Chinese cultures objectify and silence women, reducing them to nothing but “ghosts.”
